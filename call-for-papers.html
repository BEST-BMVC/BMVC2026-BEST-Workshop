Submission Details

Call for Papers

The workshop invites original, unpublished work that explores biomechanics-guided multimodal understanding of movement. All submissions should be formatted according to BMVC style guidelines and include a clear description of the scientific contribution.

Submission Guidelines

  • Authors are invited to submit original, unpublished research papers related to the themes of the BEST workshop.
  • All submissions must follow the BMVC 2026 paper formatting guidelines. Detailed formatting instructions and templates are available through the BMVC website. [BMVC 2026 LaTeX/Word template]
  • Paper Length: Submissions should follow the page limits specified by BMVC for workshop papers. Papers must not exceed 14 pages (excluding references). References and any supplementary material are unlimited.
  • Supplementary information (e.g., appendices, videos, additional results) can be submitted as a single .zip file by the paper deadline. Reviewers are not obligated to read it.
  • Submissions will be handled through the OpenReview portal. Submit your paper at: https://openreview.net/group?id=bmva.org/BMVC/2026/Workshop/BEST
  • All papers will undergo a double-blind peer-review process. Authors must ensure that submissions are anonymized and comply with the BMVC reviewing policy.
  • At least one author of each accepted paper must register for BMVC 2026 and present the work at the workshop.
  • Accepted papers will be presented as oral presentations and/or posters, depending on the workshop schedule and the number of accepted submissions.
  • Authors are encouraged to release code, models, and datasets whenever possible to promote reproducibility and facilitate future research.
  • Further details regarding presentation instructions will be announced on the workshop website.

Topics of Interest

This workshop is highly relevant to a broad, multi-disciplinary community extending far beyond core BMVC computer vision attendees. Its topics lie at the intersection of computer vision, multimodal perception, pose estimation, biomechanics, sports science, digital health/social care, physiotherapy, rehabilitation, and human movement analysis.

Beyond traditional computer vision research work, topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

  • Biomechanics-informed action recognition and pose understanding.
  • Multimodal fusion for sports analytics and health monitoring.
  • Movement forecasting and injury risk modelling.
  • Wearable sensor analysis and clinical motion assessment.
  • Foundation models and representation learning for movement data.
  • Human pose estimation, motion understanding, 3D reconstruction, and robust tracking.
  • Multimodal learning (vision, IMU, EMG, depth, physiological signals and environmental sensing).
  • Sports analytics from scientists, coaches, and industry engineers in sport technology.
  • Human locomotion and motor control in Biomechanics and neuromechanics.
  • Using pose for clinical assessment, gait analysis, injury prevention, and remote monitoring in non-lab ‘noisy’ environments in Digital Health and rehabilitation.
  • Posture analysis for risk assessment, acute/chronic changepoint detection for falls, neurological and degenerative disorders from social care, health informatics and geriatric care researchers.
  • Natural human motion interaction studies from HCI, ubiquitous computing, and robotics research.
  • Using markerless vision-based motion analysis by clinical practitioners, orthopaedists, and physiotherapists.

The topics are not limited to computer vision alone. They also advance objective movement assessment, personalised health, digital twins for humans (and their environments), clinical decision support, and real-world motion intelligence.

Submission process

Paper submission: Submit your manuscript through the workshop track on the OpenReview or conference submission portal.
Review: Each paper will receive peer review based on originality, technical quality, relevance, and clarity.
Notification: Authors will receive review decisions and revision instructions after the review period.
