Dave Yearling

Research Fellow
BTIIC, Ulster University

Invited talk

The Cost of Standing Still: Personalising Physiotherapy, Transforming Care

Brief description

Using movement intelligence to encourage compliance, support intervention and produce better outcomes for the NHS

Bio

Dave is a seasoned technical leader with a passion for solving problems using statistics, operational research and data science. His current research interests cover causal AI, autonomic decision making, computer vision and changepoint detection. Initially trained in mechanical engineering, Dave went onto study Applied Statistics and has spent the last three decades leading teams in data-driven decision-making. Solving complex challenges across both public and private sectors, creating solutions in epidemiology, logistics, service, HR, finance, networks, software production, and latterly healthcare. Committed to mentoring and collaboration with industry, universities, and government agencies, Dave is focused on transforming ideas into commercial solutions for social healthcare.
