From Training Cones to Biomechanical Context (AI for field-based performance analysis)

Bio

I am AI/ML Scientist (Sports Performance Analytics) in Loughborough University. My research focuses on deep learning and computer vision, particularly image and video understanding, biometric recognition, and multimodal information retrieval. I currently work on AI for sports, healthcare. Through academic and industry collaborations, I have developed and applied AI systems in sports performance analysis and dairy monitoring.
